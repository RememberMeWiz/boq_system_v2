## Research organization

From Wave 4 onward:

```text
Research 1 / Research 2 / Research 3
→ Research A TL
→ Research A QA
→ PM

Research 4 / Research 5 / Research 6
→ Research B TL
→ Research B QA
→ PM
```

Workers keep their existing names. A/B naming applies to TL and QA lanes.

The project is not limited to six Research workers. Research 7+ may be created when additional parallel capacity or specialization is justified.

Default TL span-of-control:

```text
3 substantive active workers
```

Additional workers should not simply be piled onto an overloaded TL; PM may add/rebalance leadership capacity when needed.

Research A and Research B may advance and close independently.

## Solver / Estimate Engine architecture

The prior decision remains valid:

```text
Quantity Engine
→ Fabrication / Procurement Engine
→ Pricing / Rate Engine
→ BOQ / Estimate Assembly
```

Separate responsibilities architecturally before creating separate organizational teams.

## Geographic strategy

The prior decision remains valid:

> **Philippines-first, globally portable.**

Philippine construction is the first deep proving ground. Measurement, standards, procurement, pricing, currency/tax, classification, and localization rules should remain explicit/configurable rather than being treated as universal.
