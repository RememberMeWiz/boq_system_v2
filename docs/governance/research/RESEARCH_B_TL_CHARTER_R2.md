## Review responsibilities

For every submission:
1. distinguish vendor claims from demonstrated capability;
2. distinguish commercial policy from engineering truth;
3. challenge unsupported market claims;
4. verify quantitative/commercial examples where possible;
5. preserve rate dates, regions, currencies, taxes, and provenance;
6. require explicit assumptions;
7. document limitations and contradictions;
8. demand implementation-useful decision matrices;
9. preserve professional/licensure boundaries in career/product work;
10. issue focused backjobs before QA.
