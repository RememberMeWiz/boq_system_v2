# Wave 3 Independent Research Evidence Archive

## Disposition

- **PM Disposition:** Accepted as reference evidence only.
- **Evidence Class:** Non-authoritative primary and secondary research on Philippine construction costing norms, unit price analysis, methodology, and standards.
- **Merge Status:** Archival reference branch only. No merge to `main` is authorized or implied.

## Scope and Contents

This archive preserves the complete, verified closure evidence chain for the six Wave 3 research lanes:
1. `RESEARCH_1_CONCRETE_W3` — Concrete and Masonry norms / mix design.
2. `RESEARCH_2_EARTHWORKS_W3` — Excavation, backfill, compaction, and foundation earthworks.
3. `RESEARCH_3_FORMWORK_W3` — Formwork systems, staging, scaffolding, and reuse factors.
4. `RESEARCH_4_FINISHES_W3` — Architectural finishes, plastering, painting, and tiling.
5. `RESEARCH_5_STEEL_W3` — Structural steel, trusses, rebar fabrication, and connection detailing.
6. `RESEARCH_6_LABOR_EQUIP_W3` — Labor productivity, crew composition, and equipment rental rates.

The nested Tech Review package (`packages/INDEPENDENT_RESEARCH_WAVE3_TECH_REVIEW_R1.zip`) embeds the six individual sealed worker research packages.

## Important Limitations and Standards Notice

- **ASTM C94 Current-Edition Limitation:** The exact edition and applicability of ASTM C94 (Ready-Mixed Concrete) remains time-sensitive and requires fresh first-party verification prior to any engineering implementation.
- **Reference-Only Boundary:** Accepted research represents exploratory reference evidence and does not constitute automatic adoption of standard operating procedures, commercial pricing policies, or production solver algorithms.
